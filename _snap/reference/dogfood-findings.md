**How to use this:** jot findings as they happen, no ceremony. Then route them:
- **bug** → also add to `ROADMAP.md` "Known issues"
- **feature idea** → promote to `features/BACKLOG.md`
- **direction signal** (which expansion matters) → feed the ROADMAP §Next expansion checkpoint

**Entry shape:**

> ### YYYY-MM-DD — <short title>  ·  [bug | idea | friction | signal]
> - **What happened:** what you did / what the CLI did.
> - **So what:** the pain or the insight.
> - **Routed to:** Known issues / BACKLOG / expansion checkpoint / (nothing yet).
